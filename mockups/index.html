Reference inventory

What we borrow, and what stays ours.

These are direction references, not templates. RelayCrew keeps its own stage rail, escrow accounting, role-based action panels, and terminal-state language.

Linear · calm technical density

Borrow the compact left rail, restrained contrast, small-caps metadata, disciplined form density, and the feeling that every control belongs to a system. Avoid copying Linear’s exact colors, iconography, or marketing composition.

Original product language

The relay, not a generic dashboard.

The distinctive object is the ordered stage rail: one funded escrow, one active specialist, an irreversible release, and an atomic next hire.

Borrowed hierarchy

Dense, legible operator surface

Left rail for orientation; a single dominant work area; right-side action panel for irreversible decisions. No card soup, no decorative charts.

Original system

Relay stage rail + escrow ledger

Stage status, provider identity, budget, deadline, and payout are read as one sequence. The rail is the product, not a generic progress bar.

Borrowed trust cues

Explicit confirmation, exact amounts

Approve/reject/expire panels repeat the recipient, exact USDC amount, and terminal consequence before the wallet prompt.

Original treatment

Cyberpunk restraint

Near-black graphite, fine grid, mint signal, amber warning. No saturated neon gradients, glassmorphism, or unreadable glow.
