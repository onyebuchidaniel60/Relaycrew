The direction.

A stricter version of Warm Editorial: fewer boxes, more typographic hierarchy, one accent per surface, and paper artifacts where the product’s trust lives.

Palette

Paper, ink, forest.

Warm ivory is the ground. Forest green carries action and settlement. Clay is reserved for refusal and refund. No neon, no glass, no gradient decoration.

Typography

Editorial display. Exact numbers.

Newsreader for the argument, Instrument Sans for the interface, IBM Plex Mono for money, addresses, and deadlines. The serif is allowed to be large; the numbers are never allowed to be vague.

Composition

The artifact is the interface.

Instead of generic dashboard cards, the landing page uses a pipeline dossier and a settlement receipt. The app screens use the same paper language, with one focal surface per view.

01
A docket, not a hero cardThe home visual is a real pipeline record with a release slip, cropped from the product’s world.
02
A bento with different kindsUI, diagram, stat, statement — not six copies of the same rounded card.
03
One active stage, clearlyThe timeline gives the review state the room and keeps paid and queued stages quiet.
04
A settlement you can readThe terminal state is a ledger with a stamp, not a success banner.

Restraint

Trust is in the details.

Fewer rounded rectangles, thinner rules, fewer shadows. Status is a chip only. Buttons are small and deliberate. The client’s decision is the loudest thing on the page.

01
Radii: 2–3pxPaper edges, not pill-shaped app furniture.
02
No invented photographyType and product artifacts carry the page; no fake people or generic agent portraits.
03
Exact amounts everywhereEvery total reconciles: 12 + 18 + 14 + 20 = 64.00 USDC.
04
Honest mockup labelsIllustrative data is marked; no transaction hashes or URLs are fabricated as evidence.
