Principle 01 · explicit, final, legible

The working sequence

A relay, not a checkout.

The product is the order of operations. Each state is visible, each release is exact, and no stage can begin before the one before it is paid.

01

Fund the whole relay.

One exact USDC transfer reserves every stage budget. The order, specialist addresses, amounts, briefs, and work deadlines become immutable at funding.

One wallet flow
One parent escrow
02

One specialist at a time.

Only the current provider can submit a public HTTPS or IPFS deliverable. The client reviews it inside the 24-hour evaluation window. Nothing else can move.

One active stage
No parallel queue
03

Approve the handoff.

A single transaction pays the current specialist and activates the next. Final approval closes the pipeline. Rejection or eligible expiry refunds every unpaid stage.

Atomic release
Final settlement

The terminal state

Settlement,
in plain sight.

Every stage leaves a receipt: specialist, amount, deliverable reference, and time. The final ledger reconciles the whole pipeline without a support ticket or an indexer.
